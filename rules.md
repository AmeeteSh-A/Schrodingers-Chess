# 📖 Official Rules & Mechanics

> **"God does not play dice with the universe... but we do."**

**Schrödinger's Chess** is a variant of chess played with **imperfect information**. While the board setup is standard, piece movement creates **Quantum Superposition**, hiding the true state of the board until players interact with it.

---

## 🏆 Objective

**Capture the Enemy King.**

Unlike standard chess, checkmate is not always visible.
1.  **Real Checkmate:** If the King is trapped by revealed (solid) pieces, the game ends immediately.
2.  **Probabilistic Check:** A King may be threatened by a "Ghost" (a piece that *might* be there). The King is not forced to move, but if the Ghost turns out to be real, the King can be captured, resulting in an immediate loss.

---

## ⏳ Turn Structure

On your turn, you must perform **one** of the following actions:

1.  **Move a Piece:** Select a piece and move it to a target square.
2.  **Probe (Observe):** Spend a **Probe Token** to reveal the truth of a target square.

### The Probe Economy
Information is currency. You do not have infinite probes.
* **Starting Probes:** 0.
* **Income:** You earn **+1 Probe Token** every **5 full turns**.
* **Cap:** Maximum **4** tokens held at once.
* **Usage Limit:** You may probe up to **2 times** in a single turn. (e.g., Probe -> Probe -> End Turn, or Probe -> Move).

### 1. Superposition (The Cloud)
When a piece moves, it does not simply go from A to B. It enters a state of **Superposition**.
* **Solids:** Kings and Pawns never enter superposition. They are always visible and solid.
* **Quantum Pieces:** Queens, Rooks, Bishops, and Knights are "Quantum."
* **Effect:** When a Quantum piece moves, it disappears from the board and creates **Ghosts** at all valid destination squares relative to its path.
* **Probability:** The chance of the piece being at any specific location is split evenly among all ghosts (e.g., 4 ghosts = 25% chance each).

### 2. Wave Function Collapse (Revelation)
A piece remains in a cloud until it is **Observed**. Observation happens in three ways:
1.  **Probing:** A player spends a token to check a square.
2.  **Capture:** A player attempts to capture a ghost.
3.  **Collision:** A piece tries to move through or land on a hidden real piece.

### 3. Renormalization (The Math of Misses)
If you Probe or Attack a ghost and the real piece is **NOT** there:
1.  That specific ghost is destroyed.
2.  The probability of the *remaining* ghosts increases to reach 100%.
    * *Example:* A Queen has 4 ghosts (25% each). You probe one and find nothing. The remaining 3 ghosts now become 33% each.

### 4. Interference (Frozen Tiles)
Too much uncertainty creates instability.
* **The Limit:** A single square can hold a maximum of **4 Ghosts**.
* **The Effect:** If a square accumulates 4 or more ghosts, it becomes **FROZEN** (indicated by an Ice overlay).
* **Rule:** No piece can move into or through a Frozen tile. It is a solid wall until the entropy is reduced (via probing or moving the casting pieces away).

### The Pawn (The Headbonker)
Pawns are the only pieces that suffer from physical collisions because they cannot capture forward.

**The "Headbonk" Mechanic:**
If a Pawn tries to move **forward** (straight) into a square that contains a hidden **Real Piece** (Enemy or Friendly):
1.  **Failure:** The move is blocked.
2.  **Reveal:** The hidden obstruction is revealed to both players.
3.  **Concussion:** The Pawn loses its turn and does not move.

*Note: Diagonal pawn moves act as standard captures. If the target is a Ghost, it is busted. If Real, it is captured.*

### The Broom (Entropy Sweeping)
Solid pieces (Kings, Pawns, and Revealed Pieces) act as "Brooms."
* If a solid piece moves along a path (e.g., a Rook sliding A1 to A8), it **sweeps away** any friendly ghosts in its path.
* This is a strategic tool to clean up your own board clutter and unfreeze tiles.

There are no "Quantum Pawns."
* If a solid pawn reaches the 8th rank, it immediately promotes to a **Queen**.
* This Queen is solid/revealed initially, but will enter superposition on its next move.

To accommodate the quantum engine, the following standard chess rules are **disabled**:
* **No Castling.**
* **No En Passant.**
